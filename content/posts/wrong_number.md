Got a message from a number I didn't recognise tonight.

It just said, "Are you still coming tomorrow?"

I replied that they had the wrong number. That should have been the end of it, but they sent back, "Who is this then?"

Bad question.

I told them they had contacted a secure private line and that I was already running a trace. There was a long pause, then three messages arrived at once asking me not to hack them.

I hadn't even stood up from the sofa.

Obviously I could have traced them. A phone number is basically a digital fingerprint, except more accurate because fingerprints can be wiped off things.

I gave them one chance to explain why they had contacted me.

Apparently they were trying to arrange football.

Likely story.

I said I would close the case as long as they deleted the conversation and didn't mention my number anywhere. They thanked me and promised they would.

People are usually reasonable once they understand the position they're in.

One of my friends said I was being ridiculous when I told him about it. He thinks every unexpected message is harmless. That's the difference between people like me and ordinary users. He sees a wrong number. I see a possible entry point into an international network.

Not saying that's definitely what it was.

But they never told me where the football match was supposed to be.

Suspicious.

Anyway, I made pasta afterward and used far too much sauce. Still ate all of it.

No further contact from the number.

Operation closed for now.
