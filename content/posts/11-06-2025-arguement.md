Some people genuinely don't know when to stop talking.

I was in a group chat tonight and one guy kept arguing with everyone about absolutely everything. Nothing serious. Just one of those people who has to correct every sentence somebody writes.

Eventually he started having a go at me specifically.

I told him to leave it.

He didn't.

Then he started with the usual stuff about how nobody could possibly know who he was because he didn't use his real name.

This is one of those things that always makes me laugh.

I didn't even have to do anything complicated. He'd posted enough stuff publicly that I could work out who he was in about fifteen minutes. Same username in a few places, same profile picture somewhere else, then suddenly you've got a pretty good idea who you're dealing with.

I sent him one message saying I knew his name.

He stopped replying.

Then he left the group.

People always think I'm exaggerating when I say I'm good at this stuff. I'm not claiming to be some government hacker or anything. I just pay attention. There's a difference.

Although I could probably do the government thing too if I had the motivation.

Anyway, after that I went downstairs and made noodles. Burnt the first lot because I was still looking at my phone.

I've started trying to cook more recently. It's going badly but at least I'm learning. Last week I managed to make something that looked like soup but wasn't technically soup.

Tomorrow I'm meeting a friend for coffee. Haven't seen him in a couple of weeks, so that'll be nice.

I should probably go to sleep soon.

It's nearly eleven and I said that at eight as well.
