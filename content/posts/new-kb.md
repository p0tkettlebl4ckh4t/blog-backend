Finally replaced my keyboard today.

The old one was getting ridiculous. Half the keys needed to be hit twice and the space bar had developed this horrible squeak that made it sound like I was typing inside an old cupboard.

The new one is much quieter, which is nice because I tend to use my computer late at night.

Spent most of the afternoon setting everything up and moving things around on my desk. I also found a pile of receipts underneath my monitor that I have apparently been ignoring for several months.

I threw them away without looking at them.

Future problem.

Had a weird conversation online later. Someone was trying to show off about knowing computers and kept saying they could "hack anyone".

I didn't say much because I'm not interested in proving things to random people, but eventually he started telling me I didn't know what I was talking about.

So I told him I could probably find his identity before he could finish typing another message.

He called my bluff.

I didn't actually bother proving it. I already had enough information to know he was talking rubbish, and there isn't much point spending an hour on somebody who thinks changing their profile name makes them invisible.

This is something I've noticed quite a lot.

People confuse knowing a few computer words with actually understanding what they're doing. Then they get very confident.

I'm probably guilty of being confident myself, but at least I've actually done things.

Anyway, new keyboard is good. That's the important part.

I also ordered a desk lamp because apparently I enjoy spending money on things I could probably live without.

At least the room is starting to look decent.

Might rearrange everything again next week.
