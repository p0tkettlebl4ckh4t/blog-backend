My train was delayed this morning.

First it was ten minutes, then twenty, then the board just said delayed as if time had stopped being relevant.

I got a coffee and sat near the window. There were about thirty other people staring at the same board like one of us might intimidate it into changing.

I could probably have changed it.

Those display systems are never as secure as people think. I didn't touch it because changing the board wouldn't make the train arrive any faster, and I don't waste capabilities just to show off.

Also there were cameras.

Eventually an announcement said there was a signalling problem. I had already worked that out. Whenever transport stops, it is either signals, weather or somebody pressing something they shouldn't.

That's basically the whole industry.

While I was waiting, I noticed a person across from me kept looking in my direction. Every time I looked back, he checked his phone.

Could have been nothing.

Could also have been surveillance.

I changed seats twice to test him. He didn't move, which means he either wasn't following me or he was very well trained. I'm leaning toward the second one because people don't usually sit that still for forty minutes.

The train eventually arrived and I got where I was going just after lunch. Bought a sandwich that cost nearly as much as the ticket and then realised I'd left my water on the train.

The bottle had no sensitive information on it.

I checked.

Long day, but at least I learned something: always bring a second drink, and never trust a man who can sit beside a vending machine without buying anything.
