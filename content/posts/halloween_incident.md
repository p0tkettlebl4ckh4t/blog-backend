I wasn't planning on doing anything for Halloween this year.

A few people asked if I wanted to go out, but it was raining and I had already bought a bag of sweets that was supposed to be for people calling to the door.

Hard to compete with that.

I watched half of a horror film, answered the door four times and then got dragged into an argument online because somebody said ZeroDays were the best hackers in Ireland.

On Halloween as well.

There are limits.

I said they were skids and some guy immediately asked me to prove I was better than them. I told him I already had. He wanted screenshots, which just shows how little he understands about serious operations.

Real hackers don't screenshot everything. Sometimes we forget.

He kept going, so I warned him that I had traced the conversation back through three countries and a smart television. He said he didn't own a smart television.

Exactly what somebody with a compromised smart television would say.

He stopped replying shortly after that. Probably unplugged it.

I could have taken the whole chat down if I wanted, but there were other people using it and I don't believe in collateral damage unless it's necessary.

ZeroDays should be grateful I have standards.

Apart from that, the night was quiet. Nobody came to the door after nine, so I ate the rest of the sweets and finished the film. It wasn't very scary, although there was one bit where a cupboard opened by itself that made me check the kitchen.

Everything was fine.

I did leave the light on afterward, but that was because I was getting a drink.

Not because of the cupboard.
